## 📋 Project Overview

As urban transportation shifts toward sustainability, electric bikes (e-bikes) have emerged as a core alternative to traditional fossil-fuel vehicles. Integrating high-torque electric motors and heavy lithium battery packs requires precise structural balance, which begins with exact geometric drafting and technical engineering drawing analysis.

This project features the engineering blueprint and geometric technical design of a **High-Performance Electric Bike Chassis and Wheel Component Layout**. By focusing on drafting dimensions, load-bearing dynamics, and stress distribution across drawing profiles, the project delivers a strong, lightweight, and aerodynamically optimized vehicle frame blueprint capable of handling everyday city commuting.

### Why This Design?
Instead of using standard bicycle layouts, this drawing features custom-engineered, reinforced **Aluminum Alloy geometry**. The technical drawing highlights an integrated down-tube housing to protect the battery, balanced structural fillets to eliminate stress points, and an optimized wheel hub profile built to withstand high motor torque profiles safely.

## 🛠️ System Architecture & Layout Mechanics

### 1. Structural Load Layout Analysis
The drafted frame functions as the central skeleton of the vehicle, tying together the steering assembly, suspension system, drivetrain, and rear wheel hub lines. The blueprint is engineered to accommodate two distinct loading profiles:
* **Static Loads:** The combined dead weight of the rider, the dense lithium battery pack, and the electric hub motor mapped onto the structural frame lines.
* **Dynamic Loads:** Sudden impact forces from road bumps, forward braking stress, and rotational torque from the motor factored into the wall thicknesses.

### 2. Frame Geometry Architecture
The technical drawing structure routes load paths across specific tube segments:
* **Head Tube Joint:** Vertical cylinder at the front profile that holds the steering fork assembly lines.
* **Top Tube:** Connects the head tube to the seat tube, providing vital lateral stiffness in the top-view profile.
* **Down Tube:** Drawn heavier and thicker than standard bikes to securely house and support the battery pack.
* **Chain Stays & Seat Stays:** Rear triangle alignment lines that safely support the rear wheel hub and axle centerlines.

### 3. Wheel Hub Layout Mechanics
The drawing models the wheel component paths to convert mechanical energy into linear movement while dampening road vibrations:
* **Dampening Profile:** The outer tyre and inflated butyl rubber inner tube lines represent the initial impact force absorption from uneven roads.
* **Transfer Lines:** The resulting load paths transfer from the outer tread to the high-tensile aluminum rim and distribution spoke lines.
* **Rotation Axis:** The force vectors move smoothly into the central hub, rotating around the main axle through a precision-bore axle hole to ensure steady acceleration.

## 📊 Engineering Validation & CAD Geometry

The frame geometry and wheel layouts were fully drafted, rendered, and evaluated utilizing computer-aided design software.

The digital blueprints focused on optimizing structural joint connections. By applying smooth radius fillets to the intersecting areas between the chain stays and the bottom bracket, the drawing layout prevents stress concentrations. CAD drafting simulations verify that the frame keeps its shape under high loads, confirming that the wall thickness of the aluminum tubes provides an ideal balance between lightweight agility and structural safety.
